# 🛡️ SafeSphere

## AI-Powered Digital Safety Risk Analysis & Safety Planning

> **Recognize the signs. Understand the risk. Know what to do next.**

SafeSphere is an AI-powered digital safety platform designed to help users identify potentially concerning patterns in online interactions and understand what they can do next.

Users can submit **text conversations, screenshots, or voice recordings**. SafeSphere processes the input, identifies behavioral indicators, combines them with contextual information, performs a structured risk assessment, and generates an actionable safety plan.

---

## 🚨 Problem

Online interactions can contain subtle warning signs that are difficult to recognize.

These may include:

- Manipulation
- Coercion
- Threats
- Financial pressure
- Secrecy
- Excessive urgency
- Emotional pressure
- Repeated unwanted contact
- Suspicious behavioral patterns

Often, these signals are spread across multiple messages rather than appearing as one obvious event.

Users need more than a simple AI response. They need to understand:

- What are the warning signs?
- Why are they concerning?
- How serious is the situation?
- What should I do next?
- Can I preserve the evidence for later?

---

# 💡 Solution
